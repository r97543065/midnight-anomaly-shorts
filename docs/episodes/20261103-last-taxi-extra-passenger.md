# 最後一班計程車的多收費用 / The Extra Passenger Fee

Story ID: `last_taxi_extra_passenger_20261103`

## 中文

凌晨一點十二分，我加完班，走出辦公大樓。雨下得很大，一台黃色計程車停在面前。 我坐進後座。司機不回頭，只說：「坐好就開。」另一側座椅空著，卻有一片濕漉漉的凹痕。 上高架後，我看見叫車 App 的乘客人數從一變成二。我重新整理，數字又變回一。 司機盯著後視鏡，平靜問：「後面那位，也跟你一起下車嗎？」我瞬間全身發冷。 鏡子裡，右後座浮出模糊人影。我猛地轉頭，座位空無一人，只有安全帶輕輕晃動。 車到我家門口。我急著下車，司機卻說：「你朋友沒下，費用照算。」車門關上前，我看見一個低頭的人。 我衝進電梯，帳單跳出：「基本車資、夜間加成、額外乘客費一名。」接著通知：「同行乘客尚未下車。」 我想申訴，電梯鏡面忽然起霧。我的背後，多了一道人影。手機最後顯示：「下一趟，已自動開始計費。」

## English

At one twelve in the morning, I left work. Rain poured as a yellow taxi stopped beside me. I climbed into the back. The driver never turned. “Sit tight.” The empty seat beside me held a damp indentation. On the highway, my ride app suddenly showed two passengers. I refreshed it. The number returned to one. The driver watched his mirror. “Is the other person getting off with you?” I froze. A blurry figure filled the mirror. I turned. Nobody was there. Only the seat belt moved. At my apartment, I leaped out. “Your friend is staying,” the driver said. “You pay for both.” The cabin light revealed a bowed head. In the elevator, my receipt listed an extra passenger fee. Then: Companion passenger has not exited. I opened the complaint screen. The mirror fogged up. Someone appeared behind me. My phone read: The next ride has already started.
